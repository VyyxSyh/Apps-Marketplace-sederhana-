## Priority issues

1. **[P1] Mobile dialog appears blank/clipped; close reportedly fails.** Screenshot shows a bottom sheet with almost no visible form content and a close icon that does not dismiss it. This blocks account/checkout/product tasks. Reproduce on phone viewport; make the sheet content scrollable above keyboard/safe area and ensure close remains reachable and operational.
2. **[P1] Cart is outside the bottom navigation.** Cart sits in the header (`index.html:28-31`), while the mobile nav has Home, Catalog, Orders, Account (`index.html:69-84`). This contradicts `PRD.md` mobile navigation requirement and makes the primary purchase flow less thumb-reachable. Put Cart in the primary bottom nav or establish an equally visible mobile destination.
3. **[P2] Navigation state is incomplete.** `updateNav()` marks only Home or Catalog (`app.js:90-97`); dialog actions Orders/Account do not reflect current location. Users lose orientation. Define active state for the actual destination or distinguish modal actions from section navigation.
4. **[P2] Product cards and controls are dense on narrow screens.** The card includes category, name, current/old price, promo, rating, sold count, store, stock and CTA (`app.js:59-72`), alongside a long category strip and five-option sort (`index.html:45-65`). Reduce secondary card details or group controls without hiding essential price/stock/CTA.
5. **[P2] Checkout total omits shipping.** Checkout summary shows item totals and total but no shipping line (`app.js:180-189`), while PRD requires shipping in summary (`PRD.md:113-117`). State the simulated shipping charge, including free/zero when applicable, before order submission.

## Persona red flags

- **First-time buyer on a phone:** modal screenshot hides the form and close control reportedly fails; cart is not in the thumb nav; checkout total lacks shipping.
- **Small seller on a phone:** dialog close/scroll failure can block product editing; modal forms and keyboard may compete for the limited viewport.
